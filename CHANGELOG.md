# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First release.
- Search: a key opens a bar over your spells and all items, recipes, NPCs, dungeons and quests of the game.
- Recommendations: a window with what is worth doing next, and hints for what matters now.
- One minimap button (left click: recommendations, middle click: search), settings in the game options under "Pylos Compass" with pages for the hints and for what is learned.
