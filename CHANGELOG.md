# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First release.
- Search: a key opens a bar over your spells and all items, recipes, NPCs, dungeons and quests of the game.
- Recommendations: a window with what is worth doing next, and nudges for what matters now.
- One minimap button (left click: search, middle click: recommendations), one settings page "Pylos Compass" with a switch for each of the two.
